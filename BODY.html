WHO WE ARE

Who is Applied Industrial Technologies

Applied Industrial Technologies is a leading value-added distributor and technical solutions provider of industrial motion, fluid power, flow control, automation technologies, and related maintenance supplies, serving MRO and OEM end-users across virtually every industrial market.

OUR TEAM

The Omni-Channel Team

Omni-Channel is the marketing engine behind Applied's reach. This is the team that puts supplier brands in front of the buyers already searching, browsing, and ordering across Applied.com, email, and social. We build and manage the brand pages, articles, and campaigns featured in this deck, backed by a 100-year track record of helping our supplier partners maximize productivity and investment returns.
